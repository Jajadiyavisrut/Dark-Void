## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive-grade operational environment for global supply chain intelligence. Built on a philosophy of "tactile instrumentation," it synthesizes the optical fidelity of high-end consumer hardware interfaces with mission-critical enterprise telemetry. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Liquid Glass & Specular Optics**: Translucent frosted acrylic layers, delicate micro-highlights, and multi-tiered specular borders emulate physical optical glass suspended in low-light environments.
- **Deep Space Immersion**: Obsidian and pitch backgrounds eliminate eye strain across long monitoring shifts while providing infinite contrast for telemetry data and spatial maps.
- **Instrumental Precision**: High-legibility sans-serif metrics paired with monospaced data markers enforce an uncompromising sense of reliability, zero latency, and absolute predictive control.
- **Target Audience**: Global supply chain directors, fleet orchestrators, and enterprise operations executives demanding instantaneous clarity across vast data fabrics.

## Colors

The palette leverages an obsidian foundation contrasted with calibrated optical jewel tones. Atmospheric illumination is achieved using restrained mesh accents rather than flat fills.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Obsidian Canvas**: Deepest obsidian (`#08090C`) and structural dark slate (`#0B0E14`). Used for underlying canvases, master viewports, and nested toolbars.
- **Primary (Electric Cyan - `#38BDF8`)**: Symbolizes active data streaming, active transit nodes, live fleet routes, and focal interactive states.
- **Secondary (Emerald Precision - `#10B981`)**: Denotes on-time operations, clear manifests, nominal thermal status, and verified node health.
- **Tertiary (Crimson Threat - `#F43F5E`)**: Communicates critical disruptions, SLA breaches, temperature excursions, and emergency geo-fencing overrides.
- **Neutral (Titanium Slate - `#94A3B8`)**: Calibrated for high legibility against tinted glass, serving auxiliary meta-labels, table headers, and structural grid guides.

### Functional Accents & Sub-Tones
- **Amber Warning (`#F59E0B`)**: Congestion, idle dwell times, impending customs delays.
- **Indigo Glow (`#6366F1`)**: AI predictive routes, automated optimization envelopes.
- **Glass Tinting**: Acrylic plates utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` layered over localized radial meshes of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)` and `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.05)`.

## Typography

The typographical framework enforces strict visual order across complex dashboards. Two complementary type systems drive the experience:

1. **Inter**: Governs headlines, narrative text, actions, and standard controls. Selected for its structural neutrality, aperture clarity, and pristine optical scaling at negative letter-spacings.
2. **JetBrains Mono**: Dedicated to operational telemetry—waybills, container IDs, ISO 6346 codes, GPS coordinates, timestamps, and key performance metrics. Monospaced tabular alignment guarantees vertical scanning integrity across real-time data streams.

### Typographic Treatment Rules
- **Display & Large Headlines**: Set with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and crisp `#FFFFFF` fills to cut through blurred backgrounds with glass-edge clarity.
- **Secondary Labels (`label-caps`)**: Always transformed to uppercase with expanded tracking (`+0.08em`) and rendered in `#64748B` or `#94A3B8` to denote technical parameters without competing with live metric values.
- **Numbers & Gauges**: Never set metrics in proportional weights; always invoke `mono-data-lg` or `mono-data-sm` with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an adaptive fluid grid built to accommodate dense real-time feeds, logistics node topologies, and macro telemetry screens.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1440px and above)**: 12-column fluid structure. Outer margins are anchored to `margin: 2rem`, and column gutters are locked to `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max-width spans `1800px` for operations center wall displays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px)**: 8-column layout. Gutters compress to `1rem` and outer margins adjust to `1.5rem`. Multi-pane split boards collapse auxiliary inspector panels into drawer overlays.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout. Gutters compress to `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`, margins to `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Data grids reflow to virtualized card stacks with horizontally scrolling metric strips.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component layouts obey strict multiples of `0.25rem` (4px base unit). Standard internal padding for primary glass containers adheres strictly to `space-lg` (24px), providing breathing room between glass rim highlights and internal content blocks. Data rows and compact data-list layouts consume `space-sm` (8px) for high density scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque stacking or drop shadows. Instead, physical presence is conveyed through light transmission, refractive layers, and subtle ambient glows.

### Optical Layer Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Atmospheric Canvas)**: Solid `#08090C` substrate overlaid with subtle fixed radial gradients: `radial-gradient(circle at 20% 10%, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08) 0%, transparent 60%)` and `radial-gradient(circle at 80% 80%, rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.05) 0%, transparent 50%)`.
- **Level 1 (Subordinate Trays / Tables)**: Translucent plate: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`. Backdrop filter blur: `16px`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Liquid Glass Cards / Navigation Bars)**: Core interactive containers. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`. Backdrop filter blur: `28px`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Inner shadow highlight: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Inspectors / Modals / Active Flyouts)**: Elevated interactive plane. Background: `rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.75)`. Backdrop filter blur: `40px`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`. Box shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.

### Specular Edge Engineering
Cards and panels feature dual-boundary definition:
1. An outer 1px structural stroke using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
2. An interior top-edge specular highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`), mimicking an overhead studio lighting rim catching polished glass bevels.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses smooth, continuous curvature inspired by precision hardware enclosures. Corners avoid aggressive boxiness while avoiding playful bubbly radiuses.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Segmented Controls)**: 0.5rem (`8px`) to ensure crisp interaction zones.
- **Glass Telemetry Cards**: 1rem (`16px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`), producing an ergonomic, framed appearance for complex charts.
- **Parent Floating Shells & Modals**: 1.5rem (`24px`) corner radius (`rounded-xl`), creating visual softness against the dark background.
- **Pill Badges & Status Indicators**: Full roundedness (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual contrast against rectangular card frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Cyan Sheen)**: Background is a semi-translucent gradient from `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.9)` to `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.95)`, text `#08090C` (semibold), with an inner specular ring `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`. Hover creates an ambient aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Liquid Glass Button**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)` with `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

### Vision-Style Segmented Pill Controls
- Encased in a single dark capsule: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Active segment transitions via a floating liquid pill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, `backdrop-blur-md`, text `#FFFFFF`, with a subtle drop shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`). Inactive segments use muted `#94A3B8`.

### Status Badges & Jewel Indicators
- Badges are pill capsules (`height: 24px`, padding `0 10px`) with low-alpha tinted fills:
  - Nominal: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` background, `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)` border, `#34D399` text.
  - Critical: `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)` background, `1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)` border, `#FB7185` text.
- Micro Status Dots: 6px circular nodes featuring localized neon halo glows (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px currentColor`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with `backdrop-blur-lg`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Focus state activates an electric cyan hairline border (`#38BDF8`) accompanied by a diffuse focus glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #38BDF8, 0 0 16px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`. Placeholder text sits at `#475569`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Toggles)
- **Checkboxes**: 18px squircle (`border-radius: 5px`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked state fills with `#38BDF8`, displaying a stark obsidian check icon.
- **Toggles**: Pill tracks (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) holding a glowing physical glass knob (`#FFFFFF`, shadow `0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`). Active state illuminates the track with an electric cyan gradient.

### Logistics Data Tables
- **Header**: Sticky bar with `rgba(8, 9, 12, 0.8)`, `backdrop-blur-xl`. Column titles in `label-caps`, uppercase `#64748B`.
- **Rows**: Alternating rows avoided in favor of spatial separation and razor-thin horizontal glass dividers (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`). Row hover triggers an instantaneous edge-to-edge specular sheen: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Metrics**: Formatted with `mono-data-sm` with tabular alignment, right-aligned for numeric payloads.
